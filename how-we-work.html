How we work

We run workshops, do research and train teams.

After every piece of work, you get a written report.

What we do

Our services

We work with companies, government agencies, schools and community groups in Singapore.

01

Workshops

We plan and run workshops, offsites and strategy sessions. Your team looks at one question from five angles. We bring disagreements into the open early, so they get settled.

You get a session plan, a facilitator on the day, and a written report of what your team said and decided.

02

Research

We find out what is changing in your market or sector. We use public sources, check every fact, and show your team how to keep the research up to date.

You get checked data, short reports and possible future scenarios you can present to your board.

03

Training

We train your staff to run these workshops themselves. They practise by leading real sessions while we watch and give feedback.

You get a training course, the materials, and staff who have run a session themselves.

Who we work with

  • HR and training teamsDecisions about hiring, training and keeping staff that look good on paper but do not work in practice.
  • Strategy and planning teams in governmentLong-term plans that must still work when the future is uncertain.
  • Polytechnics, universities and community groupsProgrammes where many different groups must agree before they act.
  • Leaders facing a new kind of decisionThere is no past example to follow, and a wrong guess is costly.

Where we work

How it works

We agree the scope in writing before we start.

  1. 1/4

    First call

    A 25-minute call. Tell us the real problem, even if it is messy.

  2. 2/4

    Proposal

    We send you the scope, a fixed fee and the dates. It is written to meet procurement rules.

  3. 3/4

    The session

    The people you met on the call run the session. We do not hand your work to junior staff.

  4. 4/4

    Report

    A few days after the session, we send a report your team can share and act on.

We are a team of three, on purpose. You only work with senior people, and you do not pay for junior staff. When a project needs more people, we bring in Singapore firms we know, and we tell you before you sign.
