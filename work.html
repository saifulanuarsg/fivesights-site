Sensing and research
A polytechnic's enterprise education team

One source of truth for a crowded training market

The team had to see the whole continuing-education market for entrepreneurship and innovation before planning new courses. We consolidated 21 separate provider scans into a single validated dataset of more than 460 programmes, fact-checked entries against live sources, and handed it over with the methodology documented so their own staff can keep it current.

Facilitation
Catapult · senior leaders

A half day watching your own judgement break down

A perception session built around a live simulation and provocation cards. Leaders watched their first reading of a situation fall apart under new information, then rebuilt it deliberately using the fivesights discipline. The session ended with each leader naming the blind spot they brought into the room.

Capability building
Community and interfaith facilitators

Training the people who hold difficult conversations

Facilitation capability for community dialogue: identity work, a structured facilitation model, and a fishbowl practicum where every participant ran a live segment and got direct feedback. Delivered across youth interfaith programmes and community seminars, for facilitators who go on to hold rooms where the stakes are real.

Facilitation
HR and L&D leads

A futures method HR could actually use on Monday

A working session where HR and L&D leads mapped the second and third-order consequences of workforce shifts with the futures wheel, then translated them into implications for their own portfolios. Participants left with their own maps, not ours.
