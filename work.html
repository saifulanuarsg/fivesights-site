Workshop

A regional app developer with nearly 20 years in the market · about 40 staff from three countries

A morning for staff to say where the company stands

The question
After a change of leadership, how do we hear honestly where staff stand and agree what happens next?
What we did
An anonymous survey before the day, then a three-hour session at the company retreat. Staff mapped the tensions in how the company works, interviewed users, and built and pitched six prototypes in mixed teams.
What they kept
A written report of what staff said, three commitments from leadership with a date to report back, and a 90-day plan. Staff confidence that they know where the company is heading rose from 3.3 to 4.2 out of 5.

Leadership

A regional technology company · two co-CEOs and a CFO

Helping three leaders work as one team

The question
One co-CEO is a long-standing member of staff. The other co-CEO and the CFO joined recently. How do the three of them share decisions?
What we did
We read their three CliftonStrengths profiles side by side with what staff told us, and showed where their strengths overlap and where the team is thin.
What they kept
A written report with five ways of working, starting with who makes the big decisions and when a signed-off decision can be reopened.

Research

A higher-education institution in Singapore, enterprise education team

One clear list of every course in a crowded market

The question
What does the whole continuing-education market for entrepreneurship and innovation look like before we plan new courses?
What we did
We combined 21 separate lists into one dataset of more than 460 courses, and checked each entry against the provider’s own website.
What they kept
A checked dataset and written steps, so their own staff can keep it up to date.

Facilitation

Catapult · more than 60 C-suite leaders

Half a day testing how leaders read a situation

The question
How far can senior leaders trust their first reading of a situation?
What we did
Sight Beyond Sight, a half-day session with a live simulation and question cards. As new information came in, leaders saw their first reading was wrong, then worked out a better one step by step.
What they kept
Each leader named one thing they had failed to see.

Training

Community and interfaith facilitators

Training the people who hold difficult conversations

The question
How do we prepare facilitators for sessions where a lot is at stake?
What we did
Sessions on identity, a step-by-step facilitation model and fishbowl practice, where one group works while others watch. We ran it in youth interfaith programmes and community seminars.
What they kept
Every participant led part of a live session and got feedback on it.

Facilitation

HR and training leads

A futures method HR teams could use the next week

The question
What do the changes in the workforce mean for our own work?
What we did
A working session where HR and training leads used the futures wheel to map what each workforce change could lead to, and what that could lead to next.
What they kept
Their own maps, with what each change means for their work.
