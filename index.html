What we do

Three kinds of work. Every engagement ends with something written.

01

Facilitation

Offsites, strategy sessions and the conversations people avoid. We surface the disagreement consensus buries, without breaking the room.

You get: session design, facilitation, and a written synthesis of what was actually said and decided.

02

Sensing and research

Desk research, interviews and data work. We build the picture from source, verify it, and document the method so your team can keep it current.

You get: validated datasets, scan reports and scenario sets your team can defend in front of a board.

03

Capability building

We train your facilitators and programme leads to run these methods themselves. Curriculum, materials and coached practice runs.

You get: a curriculum, working materials, and people who have practised, not just attended.

The method

Five pieces. One question at a time.

The mark is cut into five pieces and each one is a sight: Foresight, Insight, Sight, Hindsight, Oversight. A session takes them in that order, so a team sees the same decision from every side before it commits. Values are the paper the pieces are cut from.
