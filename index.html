What we do

Three kinds of work. Every engagement ends with something written.

01

Facilitation

Leadership offsites, strategy sessions and difficult conversations, designed and held by a facilitator who can keep a room honest under pressure. We surface the disagreements that consensus usually buries, and we do it without breaking the room.

You get: session design, facilitation, and a written synthesis of what was actually said and decided.

02

Sensing and research

Desk research, interviews and data work: market sensing, landscape scans and scenario sets for teams that need evidence before they commit. We build the picture from source data, verify it against live sources, and document the method so your team can keep it current.

You get: validated datasets, scan reports and scenario sets your team can defend in front of a board.

03

Capability building

We train your facilitators, L&D team and programme leads to run futures and dialogue methods themselves. Curriculum design, materials and coached practice runs, delivered by an ACLP-certified adult educator.

You get: a curriculum, working materials, and people who have practised, not just attended.

How an engagement runs

Scoped in writing before we start. No retainer pitch.

1

Scoping call

25 minutes. Bring the actual question, not the polished version. We will tell you honestly if this is not work for us.

2

Written proposal

Fixed scope, fixed fee, named deliverables and dates. Built to pass your procurement process the first time.

3

Delivery

The people you spoke to are the people in the room. Nothing is handed down to juniors, because there are none.

4

Written synthesis

Every engagement ends with a document your team can circulate and act on. Not a deck that dies in a shared drive.

Small by design. FiveSights is a small partnership, deliberately. You get senior people in the room, direct answers on the phone, and pricing without a leverage pyramid built into it. For work that needs more hands, we partner with established Singapore firms and say so upfront.

The method underneath

Every engagement runs on the same discipline.

FiveSights is also the name of our method: five positions a team looks from before it decides, with its values as the ground all five are tested against. It is why our sessions produce fewer surprises in implementation, and why the synthesis holds up after we leave.
