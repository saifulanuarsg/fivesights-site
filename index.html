What we believe

Many bad decisions begin as seeing problems.

Perception comes before strategy

Teams stand in one or two positions and miss what the others would show. We fix the seeing before anyone argues about the plan.

Ready for what cannot be forecast

Nobody can forecast a rare, high-impact event. We train teams to be ready when one lands, which is a different skill from predicting it.

Values belong on the table

Every session shapes values, ours included. We say so out loud, and we keep your values in plain view while the team decides.

What we do

Three kinds of work. Each one ends with something written.

We work in boardrooms, polytechnic classrooms and community halls, and the examples we use in the room come from the same places.

01

Facilitation

Offsites, strategy sessions and the conversation your team keeps postponing. We take one bounded question through each position and name the disagreement before consensus buries it.

You get session design, facilitation and a written synthesis of what was said and decided.

02

Sensing and research

Market sensing, landscape scans, horizon scanning and scenario work. We build the picture from source, check it against live pages and hand over the method so your team can keep it current.

You get validated datasets, scan reports and scenario sets your team can defend in front of a board.

03

Capability building

We train your facilitators and programme leads to run these methods themselves. Curriculum, materials and practice runs where they are the ones holding the room.

You get a curriculum, working materials and people who have practised, not only attended.

How an engagement runs

Scoped in writing before we start. No retainer pitch.

  1. Scoping call

    25 minutes. Bring the actual question, not the tidy version you would put on a slide.

  2. Written proposal

    Fixed scope, fixed fee and named dates, written to pass procurement the first time.

  3. Delivery

    The people you spoke to are the people in the room. There is nobody junior to hand it down to.

  4. Written synthesis

    A document your team can circulate and act on, sent within days of the session.

Small by design. We are three practitioners, on purpose. You get senior people in the room and no layer of juniors to pay for. When a job needs more hands we bring in Singapore firms we have worked with, and we tell you before you sign.

The method

One question, five positions, values underneath.

A session takes one bounded question through each position in this order. Each one asks something the others cannot.

Foresight

What might be coming?

Insight

What might others miss?

Sight

What is actually happening?

Hindsight

What has already happened?

Oversight

What are we responsible for?

Values

What do we protect when the choice gets hard?
