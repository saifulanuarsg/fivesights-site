Our approach

The discipline under the work.

FiveSights is the method our facilitation, sensing and training all run on: five positions a team looks from before it decides, with its values as the ground all five are tested against. It is a perception discipline, not a prediction tool. Under each position sits a working library of established frameworks, so your team reaches for the right method at the right moment instead of the familiar one.

Mode 01

Sight

What is actually happening?

Sight is present-state awareness. What is in front of you right now, stripped of narrative. It is the hardest mode because it requires you to describe what you see without filtering it through what you expected, wanted, or feared to find.

Sight is ground truth for every other mode. Hindsight without Sight becomes nostalgia. Foresight without Sight becomes fantasy. Insight without Sight becomes interpretation divorced from reality.

Questions it asks

  • What are we actually observing versus what are we assuming?
  • What are people actually doing, not what they say they are doing?
  • Where is the gap between stated process and lived experience?

Frameworks that sit here

  • Gemba walks (Toyota, Lean)
  • OODA loop, the Observe step
  • Endsley's three levels of situational awareness
  • Sensemaking (Karl Weick)
  • Cynefin probe-sense-respond (Snowden)
  • Operational dashboards and leading-indicator KPIs
Mode 02

Hindsight

What can we learn from what already happened?

Hindsight is pattern recognition from experience. Not nostalgia, not blame. The disciplined extraction of signal from your own past, your industry's past, your world's past.

Hindsight teaches Foresight. You cannot anticipate what you have not learned to recognise. Many teams confuse Hindsight with Insight: Hindsight is what happened and the patterns within it, Insight is what those patterns mean.

Questions it asks

  • Where have we seen this condition before?
  • What did we do last time, and what happened as a result?
  • What early signals did we miss, and how do we see them now?

Frameworks that sit here

  • After Action Reviews (US Army)
  • Agile retrospectives and blameless post-mortems
  • 5 Whys, Ishikawa cause analysis
  • Double-loop learning (Argyris and Schön)
  • Lessons-learned registries and decision journals
Mode 03

Oversight

What are we responsible for holding?

Oversight is governance, accountability, systemic stewardship. The view from the position of responsibility. What are the bounds of the system you are accountable for? What needs protecting?

It is the mode most teams skip, treating it as compliance rather than strategy. Teams that skip Oversight build strategies that break people or create dependencies they cannot sustain. Oversight asks the questions that keep you honest about who pays the cost of what you have decided.

Questions it asks

  • Who depends on this system functioning?
  • Where are we responsible but not in control?
  • What externalities are we not accounting for?

Frameworks that sit here

  • Three Lines of Defence
  • RACI and DACI accountability mapping
  • Stakeholder salience model (Mitchell, Agle and Wood)
  • Balanced Scorecard, used as a governance lens
  • COSO ERM, fiduciary duty mapping, ESG materiality
Mode 04

Insight

What do we understand that others might miss?

Insight is deep interpretation, the meaning-making mode. Where do the patterns point? What do they reveal about how this system actually works underneath the surface?

Insight without Hindsight is guesswork. Insight without Sight is theory divorced from reality. Grounded interpretation, not ideology.

Questions it asks

  • What behaviour are we observing, and what would explain it?
  • What constraint is actually active here?
  • What would someone need to believe for this to make sense?

Frameworks that sit here

  • Jobs-to-be-Done (Christensen)
  • Systems thinking and leverage points (Donella Meadows); the iceberg model
  • First-principles reasoning
  • Theory of Constraints
  • Mental models (Senge); the ladder of inference (Argyris)
  • Ethnographic and design research
Mode 05

Foresight

What might be coming, and how do we prepare?

Foresight is not prediction. Prediction claims certainty. Foresight claims readiness. What conditions are already in place? What forces are already moving? What might emerge from the combinations we can already see?

Foresight is strongest when it emerges from Sight, Hindsight and Insight together. Without them it is speculation.

Questions it asks

  • What changes are already underway?
  • What could disrupt the trajectory we are on?
  • What are we assuming is stable that might not be?

Frameworks that sit here

  • Scenario planning (Shell, Peter Schwartz)
  • Three Horizons (Sharpe, IFF)
  • Futures Wheel
  • STEEP and PESTLE trend scanning
  • Backcasting from a target future
  • Causal Layered Analysis (Inayatullah)
  • Weak signals and wildcards scanning
The centre, not a mode

Values

The ground all five modes stand on.

Values are what matters to you. Not what sounds good in a mission statement. What you actually allocate resources toward, what you protect when choices get hard. What you compromise on under pressure reveals your real values as clearly as what you protect.

Two teams can run FiveSights perfectly and reach different conclusions because their values differ. The framework is not value-neutral. Nothing is. FiveSights simply makes your values visible so they can be in the conversation rather than hidden underneath decisions.

Frameworks that sit here

  • Schwartz Theory of Basic Values
  • Barrett Cultural Values Assessment
  • Stated-vs-revealed values audit
  • Spiral Dynamics, as a values-development lens

Pairing rule of thumb

  • Each mode answers a different kind of question, so any framework that primarily answers that question belongs under that mode. A few, like Cynefin and the Balanced Scorecard, legitimately span two modes. That tells you where naming which mode you are in matters most.
